GPT Image label 4/4

  • Best type and layout in one shot
  • Edits keep the product and the room
  • Watch anatomy: invented a third hand

Gemini label 2/4

  • Natural people and poses
  • Small label text breaks
  • Relight edits weaker; free tier adds a watermark

ComfyUI pipeline label 4/4

  • Locked label, measured proportions
  • Same result on demand, at volume
  • Slower to set up, needs the reference set

Choice: GPT Image for ad concepts and quick edits, Gemini for people drafts, the pipeline for anything that ships.

10 / playbook

Halvane imagery standard

Light and palette

  • Natural window light, one direction, soft shadows
  • Evening: one warm practical lamp plus a cool window
  • No flash look, no colored gels
paper
greige
clay
oak
ink

Angles and shot types

  • Foot corner, three-quarter, long side to camera
  • Label always visible in product shots
  • A: product only · B: people on top of the bare mattress · C: made bed with a peeled corner
  • Leave copy space on purpose, not by accident

Never

  • A duvet or sleeping person on a bare mattress
  • A sheet tucked above the piping
  • Patch-recoloring part of the product
  • Shipping a frame that failed the proportion check
  • Other brands, logos or real people's likeness
